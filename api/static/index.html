Active alerts

Click an account to see its forecast. Hover a column header with a dotted underline for its meaning. CREDIT_EWS: a loan repayment (EMI) is at risk. DEPOSIT_ATTRITION: balance or spend is projected to fall sharply, so the customer may be leaving. Tiers: GREEN fine, SMA-0 SMA-1 SMA-2 special-mention accounts, rising severity.

Pick an account (the list shows accounts with active alerts) or click one in the Alerts tab. You get its forecast for the next 30/60/90 days with a likely range, plus its risk numbers. Hover anything with a ? or a dotted underline for an explanation.

Train the forecaster

What happens: the model reads each account's past daily values (up to 512 days) and learns to predict the next 90 days as a range (10th–90th percentile). One epoch = one pass over a batch of training windows; after each epoch it is checked on accounts it never trained on. If that check improves, the weights are saved (✓). Lower error = better.
